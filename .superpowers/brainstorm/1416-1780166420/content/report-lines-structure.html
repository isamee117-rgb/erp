Proposed Predefined Report Lines

These are the fixed sections for each report. You map your Chart of Accounts to each line. Does this structure look right?

📊 Profit & Loss — Fixed Lines
SECTION / LINE MAPS FROM
REVENUE
Sales Revenue ↳ map COA accounts
Less: Sales Returns ↳ auto (sale_returns table)
= Net Revenue calculated
COST OF GOODS SOLD
COGS ↳ map COA accounts
= Gross Profit calculated
OPERATING EXPENSES
Operating Expenses ↳ map COA accounts
= NET PROFIT / LOSS calculated
🏛 Balance Sheet — Fixed Lines
SECTION / LINE MAPS FROM
ASSETS
Current Assets ↳ map COA accounts
Fixed Assets ↳ map COA accounts
Other Assets ↳ map COA accounts
= Total Assets calculated
LIABILITIES
Current Liabilities ↳ map COA accounts
Long-term Liabilities ↳ map COA accounts
= Total Liabilities calculated
EQUITY
Owner's Equity ↳ map COA accounts
Retained Earnings ↳ auto (net P&L)
Opening Balance Equity ↳ auto (opening balances)
= Total Liab + Equity calculated
Key rules:
  • Each COA account can only be mapped to one line per report (no double-counting)
  • One line can have multiple accounts mapped to it
  • Accounts not mapped to any line are shown in an "Unmapped Accounts" section at the bottom of the report as a reminder
